Quick guide

Build a surface

Choose a preset or enter a height equation in x and y. Try cos(x) * sin(y). Choose a domain where the expression is finite and continuous. Changes update the preview automatically. Use ^ for powers, pi for π, and radians for angles.

For a parametric surface, open Torus, Catenoid or Trefoil knot and edit the coordinate tuple in the expression field. On the torus, try changing minor = 0.72 to minor = 1 to thicken its ring. A tuple may also start with r(u,v) =. Matching domain edges join at a seam. Open sheets use the wall thickness in Export STL.

Supported functions

sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, sinh, cosh, tanh, exp, log (natural), log10, sqrt, abs, floor, ceil and sinc take one argument. min, max, pow, hypot, atan2 and rmod take two. sinc(0) is 1.

Piecewise and Origin formulas

Use if(x < 0, -x, x) or x < 0 ? -x : x in a height or coordinate expression. Nest conditions for additional branches. Combine comparisons with &&, || and !.

Choose Import Origin file to load a 3D parametric Settings definition (.ofp or .xml). Surface Lab translates numeric declarations and coordinate helpers with return and if/else branches. Edit the translated tuple or find Klein bottle in the preset library.

Explore the mesh

Drag to orbit or scroll to zoom. Focus the preview and use arrow keys or + and − for keyboard control. Wireframe shows sampling density. Print mesh shows the solid geometry.

Find an extremum

In Optimizer, choose a method and starting point. Gradient descent follows the local slope, Nelder–Mead moves a triangle, and grid search refines the best sampled region. Results are bounded numerical estimates. Optimization test functions ↗

Prepare a print

For a height graph, open Export STL and choose Flat base or Thickened surface. A thickened surface adds half the wall thickness to each side along the local normal, then joins the edges. The XY span and relief set the size of the original graph. Check Solid dimensions for the final size. The catenoid uses a radial wall, and closed parametric shapes export as solids. Import the STL in millimeters and check orientation and supports in your slicer.
